Comment a word, get a free thing, finish it in the app

A comment-to-DM system for The Bali Secret, HOLD, Jupiter Walkies and Bo. Each post promises something genuinely useful. The DM delivers a real slice of it, and the full version, made personal, lives in the app.

The core idea: give the map, sell the guide

The freebie has to be useful on its own. If someone opens it and finds only an ad, they stop commenting on your next post. The split that works:

  • Free (web page): the generic version. The 7-day itinerary everyone gets, the recovery timeline, the walk chart by breed.
  • App: the version for them. Their dates, their area, their quit date, their dog, live and tracked. Swaps, maps, reminders, streaks.

So the pitch at the end of every freebie is "this is the plan for everyone, the app builds yours", never "pay to see Day 3". Blurring days 3 to 7 gets clicks once and costs trust after that.

The one Instagram rule that shapes everything

A comment only lets you send one DM. Instagram allows a single "private reply" per comment, within 7 days. You can send more only after the person writes back or taps a button, and that opens a 24-hour window.

So the first DM must not dump the link. It must earn a tap. A tap opens the window, and inside that window you can deliver, ask a question, and send one reminder.

Based on third-party developer guides (Spur, Inrō). Check Meta's current Messaging API docs before building on the exact numbers.

The flow, for every post

STEP 1PostLast slide and caption line 1: "Comment ITINERARY and I'll DM you the full plan". Pin that comment too.
STEP 2Auto public reply"Sent, check your DMs 🌴". Rotate 3 or 4 wordings so it doesn't look spammy.
STEP 3DM 1 earns a tapShort message plus one button: "Send it". This is your one allowed private reply.
STEP 4Deliver + one questionLink to the free page, then one quick question with buttons. The answer becomes a tag.
STEP 5Personal nudgeUse their answer: "Staying in Ubud? The app builds your days around it." Plus the App Store link.

Why it's built this way

  • The button in DM 1 is what makes messages 2 to 5 possible.
  • The question is a second reason to tap. It also tags them (area:ubud, area:unsure) so the last message speaks to them.
  • "Not sure yet" is the best lead you can get. It lines up exactly with the app's area-match quiz.
  • Only one follow-up message. Repeated pings read as spam.
  • The free page itself ends with the same App Store button, so people who skip the DM still convert.

Where the free stuff lives

  • Put each freebie on appsbysass.com/free/<slug>/. You already have a page per app and 21 guides there, so it's the same build. No email form: the DM is the opt-in, and a form only adds friction.
  • Every free page ends with one App Store button. Add a campaign token to each link (?ct=ig-itinerary) so App Store Connect shows installs per freebie.
  • Use the existing SEO guides as starters: the 7-day itinerary, withdrawal timeline and walk-length guides already exist and only need the free-page treatment.
  • Health freebies for HOLD and Bo: cite NHS, CDC or WHO on the page and don't promise outcomes.

Fitting it into the posting you already do

You post 28 carousels a week, and every one ends on "search X on the app store". Don't change all of them. Each week, per app:

  • 2 carousels swap the last slide for "Comment KEYWORD for the free …". Pick topics that match the freebie.
  • 1 re-cut of a winner gets a keyword. Your best posts bring the most comments, and comments also push reach.
  • The other 4 keep the App Store CTA, so you can compare the two approaches.
  • Your slide rules still apply: no dashes in slide text, no brand names, facts verified.

What to measure per freebie

  • Comments per 1,000 views shows whether the promise is wanted.
  • "Send it" tap rate shows whether DM 1 works.
  • Free-page visits → App Store taps shows whether the free page sells the app.
  • Installs per campaign token in App Store Connect is the number that counts.

After 2 weeks, keep the best freebie per app, rewrite the weakest, and add one new one.
